# KnowledgeGateNet

## 中文

KnowledgeGateNet 是一套将解谜设计绘制为规范网状图的 AI 工具包，包含**模板、工作流指导和 Skill**，主要适用于包含知识锁、信息锁及能力锁的游戏设计梳理。

使用这套工具，将你的设计整理为节点与关系清晰的网状图，配合由 Skill 生成的第二人称解谜叙述，方便设计者审视谜题内容，也帮助 AI 在后续开发中理解复杂解谜的程序逻辑。

## English

KnowledgeGateNet is an AI toolkit for turning puzzle designs into structured network diagrams. It includes **templates, workflow guidance, and skills**, and is intended for games with knowledge gates, information gates, or ability gates.

Use the toolkit to organize your designs into clear nodes and relationships, accompanied by skill-generated, second-person puzzle walkthroughs. Together, these help designers review their puzzles and help AI understand the program logic behind complex puzzles during subsequent development.
